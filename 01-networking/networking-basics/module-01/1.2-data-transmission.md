# Data Transmission

---

## Types of Personal Data

| Type | How It Is Collected | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Volunteered** | You knowingly share it yourself | Filling out an online form or profile |
| **Inferred** | Created by combining your actions | Store history used to guess your shopping habits |
| **Observed** | Tracked automatically by devices | Your phone sending your GPS location to your carrier |

> **Security Note:** Inferred and observed data are huge targets in underground markets. Fraudsters use this data to build profiles for identity theft and social engineering.

---

## Bits & Bytes

Computers only understand binary (0s and 1s). 

* **Bit:** The smallest piece of data. It has only two states (0 or 1, like an Off/On switch).
* **Byte:** A group of **8 bits** that represents a single character (like a letter, number, or symbol).
* **ASCII:** A system that converts characters into 8-bit bytes. 

```text
Example: "Daren" in ASCII
D = 01000100
a = 01100001
r = 01110010
e = 01100101
n = 01101110
```

Input devices translate human actions into binary code. Output devices turn binary back into pictures, sound, or text.

---

## Signal Transmission Methods

Once data becomes bits, it must travel over a physical medium using signals:

* **Electrical Signals:** Sent as electric pulses over **copper wires** (common in home cables).
* **Optical Signals:** Sent as light pulses over **fiber-optic cables** (fast, used for long distances).
* **Wireless Signals:** Sent using radio waves through the **air** (Wi-Fi, cellular, Bluetooth).
