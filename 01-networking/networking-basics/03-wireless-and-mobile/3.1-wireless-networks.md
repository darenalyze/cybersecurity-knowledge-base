# Wireless Networks

## Smartphone Wireless Technologies

| Technology | Range | Common Use |
|---|---|---|
| **GPS** | Global (Satellite) | Location tracking accurate to within 10 meters |
| **Wi-Fi** | Local area (~30-100m) | Connecting to home, office, or public hotspot networks |
| **Bluetooth** | Short range (~10m) | Connecting wireless accessories (headphones, smartwatches, mice) |
| **NFC** | Very short range (<4cm) | Instant data exchange (tap-to-pay, digital keys) |

> **Security Note:** Short-range wireless connections (Bluetooth, NFC, public Wi-Fi) can be targeted by attackers using rogue readers, malicious NFC tags, or open hotspot sniffing to harvest personal data.

## Wireless Connections at a Glance

* **Wi-Fi Hotspots:** Privately or publicly owned access points that broadcast wireless internet signals.
* **Bluetooth Networks:** Uses low-power radio waves to connect multiple devices simultaneously over short distances without cables.
* **NFC (Near Field Communication):** Employs electromagnetic fields for quick, close-proximity transactions.

## Quick Quiz Notes
- **Bluetooth:** A low-power, short-range technology designed to replace cables for accessories like speakers or mice.
- **NFC:** A wireless technology that allows mobile devices to talk to payment systems when held just a few centimeters away.
