# 5.4 Communication Principles Summary

---

## Core Module Summary

Network communication relies on standardized protocols and layered reference models to ensure multi-vendor interoperability.

### 1. Protocol Characteristics
* **Format & Encapsulation:** Adds required header addressing (Source/Destination MAC & IP) around raw data.
* **Size & Timing:** Segments data streams into managed frame sizes (e.g., Ethernet 64–1518 bytes) and regulates transmission flow rates.
* **Encoding:** Converts digital binary bits (`0`s and `1`s) into electrical voltage, light pulses, or RF radio waves.
* **Patterns:** Supports both reliable **Request/Response** (TCP with ACKs) and streaming patterns (UDP without ACKs).

### 2. Standards Bodies & RFCs
* **IETF (Internet Engineering Task Force):** Manages open Internet standards.
* **RFC (Request for Comments):** Official, numbered technical specifications documenting protocol design and updates.
